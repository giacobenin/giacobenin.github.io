I'm a senior software engineer who builds distributed systems and the platforms
that run them. <!-- TODO: add current employer/team if you want it public -->

Most recently I've worked in platform engineering, building the infrastructure behind
batched forecasting workflows: orchestration with AWS Step Functions and Lambda,
state in DynamoDB, and services written in Java and TypeScript.

Before that, I worked on airline schedule management systems in C#, where I relied
heavily on Domain-Driven Design to model a domain full of rules, exceptions, and
changes that ripple through the schedule.

I started out in networking research, designing middleware and protocols for mobile
ad hoc and IoT networks: peer-to-peer data dissemination, reliable multicast,
opportunistic sensing, and communications for tactical edge environments, mostly in
C++ and Java. That work appeared at venues including IEEE MILCOM, CHANTS@MobiCom,
and IEEE WF-IoT; see [Publications](/publications/).
